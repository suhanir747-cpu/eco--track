17 / PROJECT CONCLUSION

THE FUTURE OF
CLIMATE TRACKING

Digital technology gives us powerful ways to observe, measure and understand the changing climate.

Global warming tracking combines environmental science with modern digital technologies. Satellites, sensors, artificial intelligence, cloud computing and data visualization can help transform huge amounts of environmental information into useful insights.

The purpose of this project is to show how digital technology can support climate monitoring and increase awareness about the changes happening around our planet.
